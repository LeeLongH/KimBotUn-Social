# KimBotUn-Social

Big Brother Discord bot for tracking users.
- Tracking users' social score, penalties, pro-bot words and more.
- Muting people when they are low on score.
- deleting their messages when they are even lower, until they post specific sentences sent to their DMs.
- Variety of fun Welcome and Goodbye messages. (using Python Generators)
- funny Kim quotes, mute reasons and more.

Using **MongoDB** to store all data,   
and **Wispbyte** to keep it running 24/7
